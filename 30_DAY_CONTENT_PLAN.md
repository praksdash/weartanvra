# 30-Day Instagram pre-launch plan — adapted for WEAR TANVRA

## Week 1 — Brand story
Day 1 intro reel + routine/poll/Q&A stories; Day 2 why WEAR TANVRA; Day 3 day-in-life reel; Day 4 clothing-business/brand-value carousel; Day 5 design-process reel; Day 6 product mockup post; Day 7 break.

## Week 2 — Engagement
Day 8 lessons learned; Day 9 sketch-to-mockup transformation; Day 10 supplier/fabric-selection carousel; Day 11 pricing explainer; Day 12 first product introduction; Day 13 packaging reel; Day 14 break.

## Week 3 — Anticipation
Day 15 product inspiration; Day 16 product benefits; Day 17 current style/trend reel; Day 18 audience votes between colours/designs; Day 19 styling reel; Day 20 social proof or brand message; Day 21 founder/business lesson.

## Week 4 — Launch
Day 22 full product reveal; Day 23 Drop 01 announcement/countdown; Day 24 final-prep BTS; Day 25 brand values/UGC prompt; Day 26 Instagram Live Q&A; Day 27 pre-launch FAQ/pricing/sizing; Day 28 one-day-to-go teaser; Day 29 website/order walkthrough; Day 30 launch post + wearer/mockup reel + first-order story.

After launch: share unboxings, feedback, testimonials and next-drop planning. Use urgency only when it is factually true; do not claim limited stock for POD unless you intentionally limit the drop.
