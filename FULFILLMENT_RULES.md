# Internal T-Adda fulfillment notes

- Dispatch: 24–72 business hours.
- Estimated delivery: 3–7 business days depending on destination.
- Only WEAR TANVRA should submit the T-Adda order form; verify order details first.
- Prepaid supplier orders are paid before dispatch.
- COD settlement to WEAR TANVRA: D+3 to D+7 after successful delivery.
- WEAR TANVRA handles customer support; T-Adda does not communicate with customers.
- Damage/return claims: only torn/damaged/dirty and an unboxing video is mandatory.
- Designs: 300 DPI HD, PNG or CDR.
- GST: 5%; shared prices are exclusive of GST and may change.
- Up to 0.5 kg shipping: prepaid ₹68; COD ₹98 or 2.3% of order value, whichever is higher.
- If a customer rejects COD, T-Adda terms require RTO charges plus the original order value to be paid.
- Cancellations are not allowed after processing begins.
- Use only designs that WEAR TANVRA owns or licenses.
