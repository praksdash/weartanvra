Returns, Damage Claims & Cancellation

Because products are printed on demand, our return policy is limited.

  • Claims are accepted for products received torn, damaged or dirty.
  • A complete unboxing video is mandatory for a damage/defect claim.
  • We do not currently accept change-of-mind returns or size-based exchanges unless we explicitly confirm otherwise before the order is processed.
  • Orders cannot be cancelled after processing has started.

If there is a valid problem with your order, contact us promptly at tanvra.in@zohomail.in with your order details and unboxing video.
