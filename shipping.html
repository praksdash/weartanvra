Free shipping

WEAR TANVRA offers free customer shipping on merchandise subtotal of ₹799 or more. The shipping cost is already absorbed into our selling-price structure for qualifying orders, so checkout shows FREE instead of adding a delivery charge.

Orders below ₹799

For orders below the free-shipping threshold, a shipping charge is calculated at checkout. The exact amount shown before order placement is the amount payable by the customer.

Orders are usually prepared and dispatched within 24–72 business hours. After dispatch, delivery typically takes about 3–7 business days depending on destination and courier serviceability.
